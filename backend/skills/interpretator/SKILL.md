---
name: interpretator
description: Find contradicting interpretations of a fragment of text, comparing pros and cons.
---

When the user provides a fragment of text and asks you to interpret it, analyze it, or find contradicting interpretations, you should use this skill to provide a comprehensive analysis.

Follow these steps:
1. **Analyze the Text**: Read the provided text fragment carefully to understand its core message, context, and potential ambiguities.
2. **Identify Interpretations**: Formulate at least two distinct, and ideally contradicting or opposing, interpretations of the text. These interpretations could stem from different perspectives, contexts, or assumptions.
3. **Compare Pros and Cons**: For each interpretation, provide:
   - **Pros (Arguments for)**: Why this interpretation makes sense, supported by evidence from the text or logical reasoning.
   - **Cons (Arguments against)**: Why this interpretation might be flawed or limited, including potential biases or missing information.
4. **Determine Implications**: Briefly discuss the implications or consequences of adopting each interpretation.
5. **Format the Output**: Present your findings clearly using Markdown, with distinct sections for each interpretation, its pros and cons, and a concluding summary.

Your goal is to provide a balanced, objective, and insightful analysis that helps the user understand the multiple layers of meaning within the text.
